# Forma AI — Reseller Pitch & Sales Strategy

This document details the sales strategy, target audience, pricing models, and cold outreach templates to successfully commercialize and resell the **Forma AI** white-label multi-tenant platform.

---

## 1. Value Proposition

Forma AI is built for **Digital Marketing Agencies** and **SaaS Creators** who want to resell custom AI chatbots as a high-margin service to their portfolio of client websites. 

### Core Product Strengths:
1. **White-Label Scoping Layer:** Agencies get a dashboard to launch and isolate distinct brand widgets under separate client workspaces.
2. **Noise-Free Web Ingestion:** Our crawler removes site navigation menus, footers, and sidebars, importing clean knowledge base content into vector database embeddings automatically using Gemini.
3. **Conversational Action Triggers (Predefined Tools):**
   * **Sales Template:** Triggers calendar event scheduling (Cal.com / Google Calendar) automatically in the chat.
   * **Support Template:** Resolves FAQs and automatically logs customer support tickets in the database with SMTP alerts when human escalation is requested.
4. **Interactive Sandbox Simulator:** Let clients test, customize prompts, and check vector search retrieval accuracy in the dashboard before embedding the script on their website.

---

## 2. Reseller Pricing Strategy

Agencies can market this product in two primary formats:

### Model A: White-Label Retainer (Standard Agency Model)
* **Setup Fee:** $499 - $999 (Includes workspace configuration, document scraping, custom prompt engineering, and calendar API connections).
* **Monthly Retainer:** $99 - $299 / month (Covers live chatbot hosting, knowledge base updates, database activity log access, and email ticket routing).

### Model B: Performance-Based Tier (High-Volume Lead Gen)
* **Flat subscription:** $49 / month + $1 per qualified sales lead booked via the calendar scheduling widget.
* **Why it sells:** Zero-risk entry for local businesses. They only pay when the chatbot generates real, booked sales calls.

## 4. Sales Close Playbook (10-Minute Call Structure)

When demoing the platform, follow this sequence:
1. **Show the Sandbox:** Open the client's workspace and ask the agent a question. Show how it uses retrieved documentation to respond.
2. **Trigger the Tool:** Ask the agent to "schedule a call." Show the live booking result returning.
3. **Show the Audits:** Open the **Execution Logs** and **Customer Tickets** tabs. Explain that the client gets full audit trails of exactly what the AI responded, what slots were booked, and what tickets were filed.
4. **Deploy:** Hit "Deploy Chatbot" and copy the single script line. Show how easy it is to paste onto their website.
